Command Re-roll
Core
1 
CP
Any Phase
When to Use
Immediately after you make one of the following rolls for a friendly unit or model: an advance roll, charge roll, damage roll, hazard roll, hit roll, save roll, wound roll, or a roll to determine the number of attacks generated with a weapon.
What it Does
You re-roll that roll. If you are rolling more than one dice together, select one of those dice to re-roll (excluding charge rolls, which you must re-roll in full).
Quick Reference
Target
That unit or model
Trigger
Any phase, just after you make one of the following rolls for a friendly unit or model: an Advance roll, Charge roll, Damage roll, Hazard roll, Hit roll, Save roll, Wound roll, or a roll to determine the number of attacks generated with a weapon
Phase
Any Phase

Fire Overwatch
Core
1 
CP
Movement Phase
When to Use
At the end of the movement phase.
What it Does
Your unit shoots using Snap Shooting (15.09). Snap Shooting: your unit shoots as described in Making Attacks (04), with these restrictions: it can only target one visible enemy unit within 24" of your unit (and only if it is an eligible target); each attack only hits on an unmodified hit roll of 6 (irrespective of the attacking weapon's BS characteristic or any modifiers); and you cannot re-roll hit rolls. After shooting, until the end of the phase, your unit is not eligible to start an action.
Quick Reference
Target
One friendly unengaged unit (excluding TITANIC units)
Trigger
End of your opponent's Movement phase
Phase
Movement Phase

Heroic Intervention
Core
1 
CP
Charge Phase
When to Use
At the end of the charge phase.
What it Does
Resolve a charge with your unit (11.02). While doing so, before making the charge roll, you must select one of the following modes: Leap to Defend – when selecting charge targets, you can only select enemy units that made a charge move this phase and are within the maximum distance; or Into the Fray (+1 CP) – when making the charge roll, if the result is greater than 6 (after modifiers), change it to 6, and when selecting charge targets, you can select any enemy units that are within 6" of your unit and within the maximum distance.
Quick Reference
Target
One friendly unengaged unit within 12" of one or more enemy units. You can only select a VEHICLE unit if it is a CHARACTER/WALKER unit
Trigger
End of your opponent's Charge phase
Phase
